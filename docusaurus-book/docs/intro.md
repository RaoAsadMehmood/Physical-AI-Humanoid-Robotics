# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics**. This course bridges the digital brain to the physical body, integrating ROS 2, Gazebo simulation, NVIDIA Isaac, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models.

## Course Overview

This course provides a hands-on, code-first learning experience that covers the complete stack for developing intelligent humanoid robots. You'll learn to build systems that integrate:

- **ROS 2** as the nervous system for robot communication
- **Gazebo** as the digital twin for simulation
- **NVIDIA Isaac** as the AI brain for intelligent behavior
- **Vision-Language-Action Models** for cognitive capabilities

## Hardware Prerequisites

This course is optimized for two primary hardware platforms:

- **RTX Workstation**: For simulation, training, and compute-intensive tasks
- **Jetson Orin Kit**: For edge AI and real-world deployment

The content maintains strict hardware realism, ensuring all examples work on the specified platforms.

## Course Structure

The course is organized into four comprehensive chapters:

1. **Chapter 1: ROS 2 - The Nervous System** - Learn robot communication and control
2. **Chapter 2: Gazebo - The Digital Twin** - Master simulation and testing environments
3. **Chapter 3: Isaac - The AI Brain** - Develop intelligent robot behaviors
4. **Chapter 4: VLA - Vision-Language-Action Models** - Implement cognitive capabilities

## Getting Started

Begin with Chapter 1 to establish the foundation of your Physical AI system. Each chapter builds upon the previous, creating a unified architecture for humanoid robotics.
