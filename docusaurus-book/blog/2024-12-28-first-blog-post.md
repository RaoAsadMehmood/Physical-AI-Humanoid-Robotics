## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI represents the convergence of artificial intelligence with embodied systems—robots that can perceive, reason, and act in the physical world. Unlike traditional AI that operates in digital spaces, Physical AI must account for real-world constraints, physics, and real-time control.

## The Complete Stack

Our curriculum covers the complete stack for developing intelligent humanoid robots:

- **ROS 2** - The nervous system that enables communication between robot components
- **Gazebo** - The digital twin environment for safe simulation and testing
- **NVIDIA Isaac** - The AI brain providing perception, planning, and control
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models** - Cognitive capabilities that enable natural interaction

## Why This Matters

As we move toward more sophisticated robotics applications, the ability to integrate AI models with physical systems becomes crucial. This platform provides hands-on, code-first learning that prepares you for real-world deployment.
